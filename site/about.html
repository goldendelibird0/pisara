Technical write-up

How PISARA turns 6 public files into 256 decision briefs, and what every number in a brief means. Written for reviewers, engineers and analysts.

1. The problem

Every city and municipality has a Local School Board that budgets the Special Education Fund (SEF), an extra 1% tax on real property, each year. Boards decide with little school-by-school evidence. The evidence is public, but it sits in separate DepEd files from different years, and the money sits with DBM and BLGF. Nobody on a board has time to join them.

PISARA is that join, done once, the same way for every locality, and turned into the one thing a board uses: a short brief that sorts its schools by the kind of help the data points to.

2. Architecture

A five-step batch pipeline in Python, standard library only. Each step reads the previous step's output from build/ and writes its own, so any step can be rerun and inspected alone. Indicators, brief rules and sources are settings files (indicators.json, sources.json), not code.

python pipeline/run.py --accept-deped-terms

4. Joining the data

  • Schools. The enrolment file defines the universe: public schools offering elementary grades, 39,468 in all. Reading results, personnel and facilities join on the six-digit School ID. 6,113 of 6,121 schools in the reading file joined (99.87%); 8 did not. Personnel and facilities joined for 38,983 schools.
  • Localities. Schools roll up to a city or municipality by province and municipality name. Names are normalised (accents, case, “City of”, text in brackets removed) and matched to the COMPASS export. Where a city and a municipality share a name, DepEd's own wording decides. 1,565 of 1,644 localities matched. Name matching is a stopgap: the COMPASS API carries ten-digit PSGC codes, which replace it once a key is issued.
  • Special Education Fund. The same province and name match links each locality to BLGF's FY2025 SEF workbook: property-tax income, and spending by the office it was charged to. 1,570 of 1,644 localities matched, and every locality with a brief. The brief folds BLGF's twelve offices into six purposes and shows them as reported, without judging them.
  • Eligibility. DepEd's public reading file covers five regions. A locality gets a brief only when at least 90% of its public elementary schools are in that file and it has at least 5 such schools, so a brief never speaks for schools it cannot see.

5. Measures

Every measure is a ratio of two school fields, defined in indicators.json. A group's value (a city, a division, a region) is the sum of numerators over the sum of denominators, so each school counts in proportion to its learners. Benchmarks use only schools with published reading results, so a school is compared with schools measured the same way.

6. From measures to groups

Each school is checked against its regional benchmark on two questions. Is its share of learners at the lowest reading level (Low Emerging) higher than the region's? Are its learners per classroom higher than the region's? The two answers give four groups:

More learners per classroomAt or under the region
More at the lowest reading levelReading help and more roomReading help
At or under the regionMore roomNeither
  • Each group carries a kind of help to look into, written into the brief. The board decides; the brief does not allocate money.
  • Schools are listed alphabetically inside a group. No score, rank or league table is produced anywhere in the pipeline.
  • Schools with fewer than 20 learners assessed are marked, because one or two learners move their percentages a lot.
  • A school with no classroom count is treated as not crowded rather than guessed.

7. Outputs

  • Briefs: briefs/<locality_id>.html, one per locality, printable.
  • JSON API: api/index.json lists every locality; api/localities/<locality_id>.json holds a brief's full content, with benchmarks and every school.
  • CSV: schools.csv and localities.csv, described in the data dictionary.
  • A static site: no server, no database, no accounts. It can be hosted anywhere, including GitHub Pages.

8. Known limits

  • Reading results are school year 2025-26; teachers and classrooms are 2023-24, the latest DepEd has published. Crowding is enrolment over classrooms across those years.
  • The public reading file covers Regions I, III, VIII, IX and CAR only.
  • SEF figures are FY2025 and preliminary. They are town-wide totals by office, not by school, and leave out the province's share of the fund. A locality that reported no spending may simply not have filed.
  • The National Tax Allotment is shown for scale only; it is not education money.
  • The groups describe where need shows up. They do not show that crowding causes low reading, or that spending will fix it.

9. Roadmap for the fellowship

  • SEF by school. BLGF's figures stop at the town level. Local governments also file a quarterly Report of SEF Utilization under the Full Disclosure Policy. A parser for a pilot locality's report shows which schools the fund reached, beside the groups of schools.
  • COMPASS API. PSGC codes replace name matching; DepEd regional appropriations, allotments and disbursements add the national side of the money.
  • Decision records. A small record per meeting: group, amount set aside, status. The next brief reports whether it was spent, so a board's commitment can be followed.
  • Coverage. New regions and grade levels as DepEd publishes them, by rerunning the pipeline. Briefs in Filipino.

10. Rules it follows

  • Schools are never ranked, and test results are not compared across school years, as DepEd's terms of use require.
  • Derived data is shared under the same terms as DepEd's files, with credit to DepEd. No DepEd, DBM or BLGF logo, no implied endorsement.
  • BLGF figures appear inside each brief and its JSON with credit to BLGF. The full BLGF workbook is not republished; get it from BLGF's LGU fiscal data page.
  • No figure is estimated or invented. Planned parts of a brief stay empty and labelled until a real source is connected.
  • School data: Department of Education, Project Bukas machine-ready files, used under DepEd's Terms of Use and Fair Use Policy. Tax allotment data: DBM COMPASS public export. Special Education Fund data: Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF), FY2025 preliminary. This is an independent prototype. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by DepEd, DBM or BLGF.
