For scale

For scale, Pilar's National Tax Allotment for 2027 is ₱249.4 million, up 10.9% from 2026. That is the main national transfer to the locality and is not specific to education.

How to read this brief

  • Schools are compared with a regional figure, not ranked. Schools work in different conditions, and DepEd's terms of use do not allow ranking.
  • The groups point to a kind of help to look into. They do not show that crowding causes low reading, or that spending will fix it.
  • Reading results are for school year 2025-26 only and are not compared across years. Classroom counts are from 2023-24; a school with no classroom count is treated as not crowded.
  • Regional figures cover the schools in DepEd's public reading file: five regions, 6,113 schools.
  • † Fewer than 20 learners assessed. Percentages for small groups move a lot with one or two learners.
  • School data: Department of Education, Project Bukas machine-ready files, used under DepEd's Terms of Use and Fair Use Policy. Tax allotment data: DBM COMPASS public export. This is an independent prototype. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by DepEd or DBM.
